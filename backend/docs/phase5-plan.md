# Phase 5 — revised approved scope

This supersedes the earlier personal-shopper proposal.

- Sell FM LUXE's own gold jewellery; keep catalog support for later fashion products.
- Remove sourcing, personal shopping, food orders and service-management features.
- Publish priced products only; preserve historical inquiry records as drafts.
- Implement profiles, preferences, addresses, wishlists, paid-buyer reviews,
  recipient-owned notifications, customer administration and fulfilment APIs.
- Keep payment confirmation exclusively in the verified Bachs payment path.
- Do not integrate these new APIs into the website during this phase.
- Do not introduce paid services, manual transfers, refunds or identity administration.

Implementation contracts and setup are in [phase5.md](phase5.md).
Checkpoint evidence and the remaining website integration work are in
[phase5-verification.md](phase5-verification.md).
